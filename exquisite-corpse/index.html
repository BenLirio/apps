Exquisite Corpse

A field-guide for creatures that ought not exist.

Two players. One phone. One creature you will invent together — without ever seeing what the other drew.

Player One draws the upper half. The device passes. Player Two completes the specimen blind. A name is bestowed.

Player One sketch the upper half. Head, shoulders, the usual pretensions. Pass when finished.

the seam

 

Pass the phone.

Player Two — when you have it, tap below. No peeking.

Player Two complete the lower half. Meet the small marks at the seam — those are where Player One left off.
