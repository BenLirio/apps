The Met Gala Step-and-Repeat

User answers 5 absurd quick-tap chip questions about their week (mood, breakfast, conflict, song stuck in head, recurring dream) and gets escorted down a fake red-carpet hallway as paparazzi flashes pop. At the end: a fashion-critic verdict card with a named look ('Distressed Cathedral by the House of Tuesday'), the user's outfit dossier, and a one-line review in the voice of a snarky Vogue commentator. Output is a styled gala-arrival ID badge / press-pass artifact with their look-name, gown house, and verdict score on a 1-100 'Cultural Resonance' scale.

Lands in the genuinely empty navigate × archetype-reveal cell. Borrows the Met Gala (May 4) cultural moment with a one-week lift window — timely without being a clone. Solves the wizard-depth watchlist by collapsing 5 chip-taps into a single navigate-down-the-carpet animation (~30s total). Five chip axes give input-resolution coupling enough lift to clear the hard rejection rule. LLM writes one personalized critic sentence as a payoff, not as the medium of the experience. Premise legible in 5s ('what would your Met Gala arrival look like'). Stance: mock-fashion-critic, matching the petty-small-claims success register. Every named look is flattering-absurd. Solo-UAT-able single-player journey; mobile-native chip-tap navigation.
