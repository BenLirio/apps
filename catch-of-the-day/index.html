A naturalist's survey of the lake, conducted by tap and patience. Cast the line. Endure the struggle. Bring the specimen to plate.

  • I. Hold to draw the rod. Release at strength.
  • II. Tap to give slack when the line strains.
  • III. Mistime twice and the specimen flees.

No bait will be wasted. The Survey records every cast.
