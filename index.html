Stowaway Manifest

The user picks one of 8 absurd pixel-art passenger archetypes (The Diplomat's Cat, The Carpetbagger, The Stowaway Monk, The Calendar Bandit, etc.) to slip aboard today's departing zeppelin, then watches a 60-second seeded voyage play out — storms, customs checks, a kraken, a rival airship. At landing, the Customs House issues an official tri-folded Daily Manifest: the chosen passenger's stamped fate at port, three named customs inspectors' verdicts ('Spiritually Inadmissible Cargo', 'Released Pending Vibes'), a hand-drawn route map of what happened, and a featured cargo audit. Same passenger × same date = same voyage. Different passenger picks visibly change the journey. Mock-bureaucratic typewriter aesthetic, faded customs stamps, period maritime ink. Share is the manifest PNG plus a URL fragment that replays the same voyage.

The Walking Tour Confession

A fake AI surveillance van follows you on a procedural pixel-art map for 90 seconds. You navigate by clicking 8-10 numbered street corners (the route IS the input — diner > pawn shop > vape store > dog park > ATM). At each stop the screen pauses and a deadpan plainclothes detective dictates a single line into your case file about what happened there. At the end you receive a stamped Surveillance Report: a named civilian-archetype verdict, the full route map as Exhibit A, and 8 numbered detective notes quoting your route choices back. Shareable: the recipient sees your exact route + report, then clicks through their own.
